Questions

What ages are the books for?Ages 2 to 5. Each book is marked Level 1, 2 or 3: Level 1 has one giant shape per page for the youngest, Level 3 has small simple scenes.
Do I get a physical book?No: it's a digital download (PDF). Nothing is shipped. You print it at home or at a print shop.
Which paper do I need?Regular printer paper. Every book includes US Letter and A4.
Can I print it more than once?Yes, as often as you like for your own family.
Can I add my child's name?Yes: choose "Personalized with name" on a book's Etsy page. The name goes on the cover, the "This book belongs to" page and the certificate.
